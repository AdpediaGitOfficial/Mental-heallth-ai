15 Guidelines

◎ Do

  • Let cards float on the gradient with soft shadows — no hard borders.
  • Use green/amber/red only to signal health range or risk, never decoration.
  • Keep Mind (blue) and Body (emerald) rings equal size, always paired.
  • Pair every icon with a tint tile in its own semantic colour.
  • Reserve the blue gradient for the single hero card per screen.

✕ Don't

  • Don't place the blue gradient behind body text or multiple cards.
  • Don't mix status colours (e.g. a green value with an amber sparkline).
  • Don't shrink one anchor ring below the other — parity is a hard rule.
  • Don't use pure black (#000) or hard 1px borders — use Ink + shadow.
  • Don't stack more than one high-emphasis (red/gradient) element per view.
